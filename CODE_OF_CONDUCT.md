# Code of conduct

Short version: be decent.

This is a hobby hardware project maintained by one person in their spare time.
Contributions, bug reports and questions are welcome in English or Chinese.

- Critique code, not people. "This variable is shadowed on line 412" is fine; "your
  code is a mess" is not.
- Assume good faith. Most of what looks like a mistake here is a deliberate trade-off
  for a 240×135 screen and a 8 MB part.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Keep reports on topic: a bug report should say what you flashed, what you saw, and
  what you expected.

If something in this repository makes you uncomfortable, open an issue or contact the
maintainer through GitHub. Repeated bad behaviour gets a block.

---

## 行为准则（中文）

简单说：做个正常人。

这是个人利用业余时间维护的硬件玩具项目，欢迎中英文的贡献、issue 和提问。

- 批评代码可以，攻击人不行。
- 默认善意——这里很多看起来"奇怪"的地方，是为了 240×135 屏幕和 8MB 硬件的刻意取舍。
- 不接受骚扰、歧视、人身攻击。
- 报告请切题：说清楚刷的哪个版本、看到了什么、期望是什么。

维护者保留拉黑屡次越界者的权利。
